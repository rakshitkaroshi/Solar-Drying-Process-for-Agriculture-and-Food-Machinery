# Solar Drying Process for Agriculture and Food Machinery

## Project Overview

The **Solar Drying Process for Agriculture and Food Machinery** is a sustainable drying system designed to preserve agricultural and food products using renewable solar energy.

The system uses a solar collector, solar absorber, drying chamber, air blower, thermal energy storage unit, and weighing scale to achieve efficient drying. It utilizes both direct and indirect solar heating mechanisms to remove moisture from agricultural products while reducing dependence on conventional energy sources.

A data logger is integrated to record weight measurements at regular intervals of 2 minutes, helping monitor moisture loss during the drying process.

## Working Principle

The system operates using the following process:

1. **Solar Energy Collection:** Solar panels and solar collectors capture solar radiation.
2. **Heat Generation:** The solar absorber converts solar radiation into thermal energy.
3. **Air Circulation:** An adjustable-speed blower pulls air through the absorber and directs heated air into the drying chamber.
4. **Product Drying:** Agricultural products are placed inside the drying chamber, where heated air removes moisture.
5. **Thermal Energy Storage:** Excess heat is stored in the thermal energy storage unit using phase change material (PCM).
6. **Weight Measurement:** An integrated weighing scale measures the product's weight during the drying process.
7. **Data Logging:** Weight measurements are recorded at 2-minute intervals using the data logger.
8. **Drying Completion:** The process continues until the required moisture reduction is achieved.
